Search Engine Optimization has evolved. Google's algorithm heavily penalizes slow, JavaScript-bloated websites with high **Time to First Byte (TTFB)** and poor **Largest Contentful Paint (LCP)**.

Here is why **Astro** has become the premier choice for modern SEO agencies and high-converting marketing sites.

## 1. Zero JavaScript by Default
Traditional React or Next.js applications ship megabytes of hydration JavaScript to the client browser. Astro strips away all client-side JS by default, compiling your pages into clean, static HTML and CSS.

## 2. Islands Architecture
If your site needs interactive components (like search bars or contact forms), Astro uses **Islands Architecture**. Only the specific interactive component loads JavaScript—leaving the rest of the page fast and lightweight.

## 3. Pre-Rendered Speed & Instant Indexing
Search engine crawlers (Googlebot) prioritize pages that render instantly. With Astro pre-rendering pages at build time or on edge servers (Vercel/Cloudflare), your pages index faster and rank higher.
