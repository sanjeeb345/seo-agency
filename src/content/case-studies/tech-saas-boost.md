## The Challenge
CloudScale SaaS had great product feature pages, but organic impressions were stagnant. Their React-based legacy site suffered from slow LCP times (4.2s) and over 1,200 orphan pages missed by Googlebot.

## Our Strategy
1. **Migration to Astro & Edge CDN:** Reduced LCP from 4.2s to 0.6s.
2. **Technical SEO Audit:** Fixed crawl errors, canonical mismatches, and XML sitemaps.
3. **Programmatic Content:** Scaled targeted comparison landing pages.

## Results
* **+340%** Organic Traffic increase
* **#1 Rank** for 14 primary commercial intent keywords
* **$120,000+** in new ARR driven from organic search
